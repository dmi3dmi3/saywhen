SayWhen now turns on the languages you write in by itself, and it no longer writes to just one calendar.

• An alias in the phrase: "lunch #work" sends the event to another calendar.
• Tasks in Tasks.org: its lists appear next to your calendars.
• Settings on one screen.
• Time with a dot: "16.00" is read as 16:00.
• "+ event" shortcut for launchers.
• Understands "tmrw", "tmr" and "tdy" in English.

Details are on the GitHub release page.
