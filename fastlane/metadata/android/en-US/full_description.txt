No forms, no fields. Just say when.

Type an event the way you'd say it, in English or Russian:

• Gym every tuesday and friday at 9
• Movie tomorrow at 11 for an hour and a half
• Dentist june 3 at 12:30

SayWhen parses the phrase as you type — date, time, duration, recurrence — shows a live preview of what it understood, and writes the event straight to the calendar you chose. Tap the home-screen widget, type, done.

Private by construction:

• No account of its own, no server, no analytics, no identifiers
• No network access at all — the app doesn't even declare the INTERNET permission
• Two permissions total: read your calendar list, write the event you asked for

Events go to your calendar — nowhere else. If your calendar belongs to a synced account, its own sync engine uploads the event, exactly as with any other calendar app.
